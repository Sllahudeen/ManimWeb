About Me

I am a physics enthusiast with a strong interest in theoretical physics, mathematical structures, and computational methods. I enjoy exploring how mathematical ideas describe the physical world and how complex concepts can be communicated through clear visual representations.

This website is a collection of animations I develop using Python and Manim. Through these projects, I aim to connect mathematical reasoning with visual intuition and explore physical systems in an interactive and engaging way.
